Requester Dashboard Help

View Requests

When you click on "Requester", you will land on the View Request Page. Here, you can see the job requests made by anyone.

Add Job Request

To add a job request, click on the "Add Job Request" button. Fill in the necessary details and submit the request.

View and Select Candidates

On the View Request Page, you can view candidates by clicking on the "Candidates" button next to each request. This will open a popup where you can see the list of candidates added for the particular request. You can filter the candidates for the interview process by changing their status to "selected".

Provide Feedback

After the interview process, you can provide feedback for the candidates. Click on the "Add Feedback" button in the popup, which will redirect you to the Feedback Page. Here, you can enter the feedback, score, and status for the interview round. The feedback provided will be visible to the recruiters in the Recruiter Dashboard under the "Feedback Status List".
